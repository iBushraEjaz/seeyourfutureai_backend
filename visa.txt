International Student Visa Requirements
48 destinations • Four study levels • Optional scholarship guidance
Global edition · 20 November 2026
Who this guide is for
International applicants of any nationality planning undergraduate, taught master’s, research master’s or PhD
study. Nationality is not assumed. Requirements that depend on nationality, residence, age, course or
applicant category are labelled as conditional.
How to use this guide
Your app’s existing core checklist should remain separate. This guide focuses on destination-specific items
and optional scholarship extras. Do not show a scholarship checklist as mandatory to applicants who are
self-funded.
Important: Visa rules and scholarship calls change. This is a structured planning baseline, not a fully verified
live legal checklist for every consulate. Confirm the latest checklist with the destination government, embassy
or consulate, and the university before publishing or applying.
Core checklist: keep separate in the app
The uploaded visa screen already contains shared academic and application items. Keep those in the core
layer rather than repeating them for every destination. Typical shared items include passport, academic
certificates and transcripts, admission evidence, CV, statement of purpose, language evidence,
recommendation letters and financial evidence. The exact core list should follow the applicant level and your
existing screen implementation.
Important funding rule
A scholarship award does not automatically remove the immigration financial requirement. It may satisfy all or
part of it only when the award is recognised by the destination authority and clearly states the amount,
duration and covered costs. If the award is partial, the applicant may need evidence for the remaining amount.
Conditional documents
Examples include APS for certain qualification origins, UK ATAS for specified courses and nationalities, UK
TB screening based on residence history, and attestation letters required only for certain Canadian study
permit categories. These must be triggered by eligibility logic, not displayed as universal requirements.
Country-by-country reference
Country-specific entries below are planning prompts. Where a destination has a named document or process, it is listed. Other
destinations require the applicant to follow the national student visa or residence permit checklist for their case; do not infer that every
possible document is required.
1. Albania (AL)
Bachelor’s: Guardian Consent (age dependent): Applicants under the legal age may need parental consent and guardian details.
Taught Master’s: Degree Recognition (conditional): Provide degree recognition or attestation only where the destination or institution
requires it.
Research Master’s: Supervisor Confirmation (programme dependent): Provide a supervisor acceptance or host confirmation when the
programme requires one. Research Proposal (programme dependent): Submit the research plan or project description required by the
institution. Funding Confirmation (if funded): Provide the scholarship, assistantship, employment or stipend confirmation if it funds your
stay.
PhD: Supervisor Confirmation (programme dependent): Provide a supervisor acceptance or host confirmation when the programme
requires one. Research Proposal (programme dependent): Submit the research plan or project description required by the institution.
Funding Confirmation (if funded): Provide the scholarship, assistantship, employment or stipend confirmation if it funds your stay.
Optional scholarship examples: Government or university merit scholarships.
Verification: Check the official immigration/foreign ministry portal and the scholarship provider’s current call before use. The app should store the source
URL and last-checked date after verification.

Scholarship eligibility warning
Age limits, work experience, nationality restrictions, eligible courses, referee counts, return-home conditions

Implementation notes for the app
• Use country ISO codes as stable keys, not display names or city names.
• Keep the four levels distinct: bachelors, taught_masters, research_masters and phd.
• Keep scholarship lists optional and separate from country visa requirements.
• Use applicability values to conditionally display items such as nationality_dependent, residence_dependent, course_dependent,
age_dependent and if_awarded.
• Do not duplicate core checklist items in country data. If a country-specific financial item replaces a core funds item, ensure the
replacement key exists for that applicant level; otherwise append it.
• Do not show old financial amounts as permanent values. Store amount, currency, source URL, effective date and last_checked
separately, and refresh them from official sources.
• Each country entry should be completed with official source URLs and a last_checked date before the data is presented as verified.
